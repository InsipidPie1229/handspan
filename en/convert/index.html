    When the current DPI and the game's sensitivity steps cannot get within 3% of the target, we show the closest value and its error and leave it out of the recommendations. 3% is a temporary tolerance, not a verified perceptual threshold.

    Check it in game

    1. Enter the sensitivity in game and make sure mouse acceleration (including Enhance pointer precision) is off.
    2. Put a ruler next to the mouse or mark a start point on the pad, and aim at a distinct point on screen.
    3. Slide the mouse slowly sideways until you turn exactly once and land on the same point, and measure the distance.
    4. Compare it with the cm/360 above. If it is off by more than 3%, check the DPI setting, acceleration and the game coefficient.

    This matches horizontal hip-fire rotation only. ADS and scope multipliers, field-of-view settings and vertical multipliers work differently in each game and need separate matching.

    Formula

    cm/360 = 914.4 ÷ (DPI × sensitivity × yaw). Yaw is the angle turned per mouse count at sensitivity 1. 914.4 is 360° × 2.54.

    Not included

    ADS and scope multipliers, mouse acceleration, and per-game field-of-view settings are not included. Only horizontal hip-fire rotation is matched.

    Verification

    Every value is an estimated conversion that has not been measured in the real game. The number of sources is shown, but converter sites may copy the same list, so their independence is not verified. Deadlock, Marvel Rivals and PUBG (conflicting values) and Battlefield 6 (non-linear sensitivity) are left out until resolved.
