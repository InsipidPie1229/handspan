About and sources

What is stored, what is requested from elsewhere, and where the numbers come from.

Storage and external requests

  • No analytics or ads of our own. Practice records and settings stay in this browser (localStorage) and are not sent to a server.
  • Fonts (Archivo, IBM Plex Mono, IBM Plex Sans KR) are served from this site. No requests go to Google or other external servers. Font licenses (SIL OFL 1.1) are in assets/fonts/LICENSES.md.
  • The hosting service that serves the site may keep ordinary access logs.

Measurement and its limits

  • Results include browser, mouse and display latency. They are not the same as in-game performance.
  • The finder gives a starting range, not an answer. Try it in the real game for a few days before settling.
  • The 3% target tolerance and the 0.25 s stall threshold are temporary values, not verified ones.
  • An end-to-end run with a real mouse has not been verified yet. The measurement logic has only been checked with automated tests.
