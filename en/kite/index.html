What it measures

Accuracy while alternating between a move point and an attack target, order errors from pressing the wrong button, clicks on the other target, and the gap between successful clicks. These numbers measure different things, so they are not combined into one score.

How it differs from a real game

This practice does not reproduce any game's attack speed, movement, or cancel behavior. It uses only original shapes, with no characters or maps from a specific game. It only practices alternating move and attack clicks with the cursor.

Comparing runs

Only runs with the same settings, including the button assignment, are shown together in this browser. Runs from another PC or other settings are not compared.
