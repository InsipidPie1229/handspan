Game age test

Six mini-games measure reaction, aim, tracking, timing, decisions and memory, then give you a just-for-fun 'game age' and gamer type.

Ready?

Six games in a row, about 3-4 minutes. If you switch tabs or leave the window, that game starts over.

  • Game age is handspan's own just-for-fun grade. It does not estimate your real age or health.
  • Results include mouse, display and browser latency, so the same person can score differently on another device.
  • Nothing is sent to a server. A share link contains only your measurements.

    How it is calculated (rules v1)

    Each measurement is mapped to 0-100 by straight lines between the points below (values outside use the end points). The aim score is multiplied by (0.5 + 0.5 × hit rate) and the color score by accuracy². The total is the average of the six, and game age = 16 + (100 − total) × 0.5 (16 to 66). The type is All-rounder when the total is 75+ and all games are within 20 points, Laid-back player when the total is under 35, and otherwise your best game. The points are handspan's own choices, not research or population statistics.
